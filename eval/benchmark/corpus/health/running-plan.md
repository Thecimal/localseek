# Running plan, 10K in the spring

Weeks 1 to 3: replace all runs with cycling or swimming because of the knee. Week 4: walk-run intervals, two minutes running and one minute walking, three times a week. Weeks 5 to 8: build continuous running to 30 minutes. Weeks 9 to 12: add one tempo session per week. Stop and rest if the knee pain goes above 3 out of 10.
