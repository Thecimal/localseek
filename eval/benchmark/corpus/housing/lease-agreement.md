# Residential Lease Agreement

## 1. Parties and premises
This lease is made between Tobias Okafor (the Landlord) and Mara Veld (the Tenant) for the apartment at 14 Orchard Row, Unit 3B. The Landlord can be reached on 555-0142 for all non-emergency matters. The premises include one assigned parking space, number 12, and a storage cupboard in the basement.

## 2. Term
The initial term is twelve months beginning on 1 January 2024. After the initial term the lease continues month to month unless either party ends it under section 11.

## 3. Rent and payment
Monthly rent is $1,450, due on the first day of each month by bank transfer to the account named in the cover letter. A late fee of five percent of the monthly rent applies when a payment is more than five days overdue. Rent may change only by a written addendum signed by both parties, and any increase takes effect no earlier than the first day of the following January.

## 4. Security deposit
The Tenant has paid a security deposit equal to one month's rent. The Landlord holds it in a separate account and returns it within thirty days after the Tenant moves out, less the documented cost of any damage beyond normal wear and tear. Normal wear includes faded paint, small nail holes and light carpet wear.

## 5. Utilities and services
The Tenant pays for electricity and internet service. The Landlord pays for water, trash collection and the building insurance. Heating is supplied by the building boiler and is included in the rent from 1 October to 30 April.

## 6. Maintenance and repairs
The Landlord keeps the heating system, plumbing and structure in working order and responds to written repair requests within seven days. The Tenant keeps the unit clean, reports leaks and mould promptly, replaces light bulbs and smoke alarm batteries, and does not make structural changes. Repairs needed because of the Tenant's negligence are charged to the Tenant at cost.

## 7. Entry and inspections
The Landlord may enter the unit with twenty-four hours notice to carry out repairs or inspections, or at any time in an emergency. A move-in inspection report signed by both parties forms part of this lease, and a move-out inspection follows the same checklist item by item.

## 8. Quiet hours and shared areas
Quiet hours are from 22:00 to 07:00 on every day of the week. The Tenant keeps shared stairways free of belongings and uses the bicycle room only for bicycles. Participation in the building cleaning rota is scheduled by the Landlord at the start of each quarter, and the Tenant agrees to swap turns with neighbours only by mutual arrangement.

## 9. Pets and smoking
Cats and dogs are permitted with the Landlord's written approval. A pet deposit of $300 is due before the animal moves in, for each cat or dog; it is refundable on the same terms as the security deposit. Reptiles, exotic animals and more than two pets in total are not permitted. Smoking is prohibited inside the unit and on the balcony.

## 10. Subletting
The Tenant may not sublet the unit or any room in it without the Landlord's prior written consent. Where consent is given, a sublease may last no longer than ninety days in any twelve-month period, and the Tenant remains fully responsible for rent and damage during the sublease.

## 11. Termination
After the initial term either party may end the lease by giving sixty days written notice. During the initial term the Tenant may leave early only by paying a fee equal to two months' rent. The Landlord may end the lease early only for serious breach, such as non-payment for more than thirty days.

## 12. Governing law
This lease is governed by the housing law of the jurisdiction where the premises are located.
