# Apartment lease notes

Signed the twelve-month lease on the second-floor flat. The landlord agreed in writing to repair the heating system before the first cold spell and to replace the two broken radiators in the bedroom.

## Deposit

The security deposit is one month's rent, held in a separate account and returned within thirty days of move-out, minus documented damage.

## House rules

No pets without permission. Quiet hours start at ten in the evening. Subletting requires written consent from the landlord.
