# Sourdough routine

Feed the starter twice a day when it lives on the counter, and once a week when it lives in the fridge. Use equal weights of flour and water, discarding all but a spoonful of the old starter first.

## Baking day

Mix the dough at 75 percent hydration, fold it every thirty minutes for two hours, then shape it and let it rest overnight in the fridge. Bake in a preheated Dutch oven for twenty minutes covered and twenty-five uncovered. Good oven spring depends on a strong, bubbly starter.
